# Download Island Polish

**Status:** Completed  
**Created:** 2026-09-14  
**Plan type:** Feature / downloads UI  
**Related product spec:** [`../../product-specs/downloads.md`](../../product-specs/downloads.md)  
**Related reliability authority:** [`../../RELIABILITY.md`](../../RELIABILITY.md)

## Objective

On iOS, keep download progress visible while Vanmo is in the foreground, then hand it to the system Dynamic Island when the app backgrounds. Improve the no-island fallback landing, lock-screen deep link, expanded Live Activity actions, and completion banner.

## Scope

- Foreground in-app Compact (`569:38`) and Expanded (`570:259`) over the hardware island; Compact tap expands, Compact ring is not a pause control, Compact paused shows a resume icon (`572:14`); island phones never show the status-bar fallback bar
- Detail enqueue flies the material capsule to the hardware island, aligns to `islandFrame`, then morphs once to Compact; `restoreAndResume` and Reduce Motion skip the flight and show Compact
- System Live Activity compact/expanded replica plus pause / resume via App Intents
- Lock-screen / banner `widgetURL` to `vanmo://downloads`
- Completion `alertConfiguration`
- No-island: land, short spring, then fade in the top bar
- iOS 26+ liquid glass on the fallback bar only

## Out of Scope

- Changing `DownloadManager` queue semantics
- macOS hero animation
- Files-browser download hero
- Raising QUALITY_SCORE without a recorded Compact/Expanded walk
- Expanding physical-device screenshot and recording coverage beyond the operator walks already recorded
- Raising QUALITY_SCORE Downloads or the whole snapshot

## Verification

1. `swift test --package-path Packages/VanmoCore`
2. `./scripts/check-architecture-guards.sh`
3. `./scripts/check-harness-docs.sh`
4. `./scripts/check-app-build.sh ios-simulator` (serial, not parallel with macos)
5. Manual fixture walks (`VANMO_DEBUG_HERO_WALK`) on iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone SE

## Risks

- Simulator Live Activities are requested in the foreground; a 2026-09-17 operator walk confirmed they do not steal Compact/Expanded hits and that this request is what lets the scene absorb into the hardware island
- Simulator expanded long-press and haptic alerts can be incomplete
- `vanmo://downloads` must not intercept `vanmo://oauth/...`
- ActivityKit compact leading/trailing cannot draw the free-floating 262pt capsule; background stays leading/trailing replicas of `569:38`
